## 3. Vendor Dashboard Contribution

The Vendor Dashboard was the main part of my assigned contribution. The purpose of the dashboard is to provide restaurant owners with a centralized overview of their restaurant's performance.

The SRS specifies that the Vendor Dashboard should contain several important areas, including a Rating Summary Panel, Review Count Overview, Profile View Statistics, Engagement Metrics, Recent Activity Summary, Performance Trend Charts, and a Notification and Alert Section.

These requirements provided the basis for organizing the dashboard around the information that vendors would need most frequently. Instead of requiring vendors to examine different parts of the system separately, the dashboard is intended to provide a consolidated view of important performance information.

The dashboard is also connected to the overall analytics workflow described in the SRS. Platform activity data is collected from different modules, review and engagement information is retrieved from the Moderation & Review System, and visibility and interaction data comes from the Discovery System. The Analytics Engine processes and aggregates this information before the Vendor Dashboard presents charts, summaries, and reports to the restaurant owner.

This helped me understand the importance of presenting complex information in a simple and organized manner.

## 4. Analytics Contribution

The second major part of my contribution was the Analytics component. The purpose of the analytics functionality is to allow restaurant owners to monitor their performance and understand trends over time. 
The SRS identifies several analytics requirements, including displaying the restaurant's average customer rating, rating distributions and trends, the total number of customer reviews, profile views, customer engagement metrics, activity reports, and restaurant performance trends.

The analytics requirements also include the ability to filter reports by date range, export activity reports, compare current performance with previous periods, and highlight significant increases or decreases in performance.

The Analytics Report Page described in the SRS includes detailed analytics reports, date-range filters, performance comparison charts, review and rating analysis, engagement statistics reports, and report export functionality.

My work on this area was therefore based on providing vendors with an organized way to understand their restaurant information rather than simply displaying raw data. The goal of analytics is to make the information useful for evaluating performance and supporting business decisions.

## 5. Technical Contribution and Integration

The Vendor Dashboard and Analytics component is part of the larger Vendor Portal.
